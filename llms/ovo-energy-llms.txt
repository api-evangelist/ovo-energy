## What a developer or agent needs to know first

- There is nothing to sign up for. No developer portal, no API key request form, no partner application, no data licence, no accreditation pathway.
- `api.ovoenergy.com` resolves and is live behind Cloudflare but answers every path with a bare `text/plain` 404: "No context-path matches the request URI." It is a gateway with no public context path.
- The only real consumer data endpoint found is undocumented and unsupported: `https://smartpaymapi.ovoenergy.com/usage/api/half-hourly` returns HTTP 401 JSON to an anonymous caller and serves half-hourly smart-meter consumption only to a signed-in OVO customer session. OVO staff have said on the company forum that these interfaces are for OVO/Kaluza internal use, are not public-facing, and may change or be withdrawn without notice.
- Great Britain mandated the metering INFRASTRUCTURE (Smart Energy Code, licensed Smart DCC), not a consumer data right. There is no GB equivalent of the Australian Consumer Data Right or Ontario's Green Button that would compel OVO to expose customer data through an API.
- Do not attribute Australian CDR obligations to this company. OVO Energy Pty Ltd (Australia) is a designated CDR energy data holder, but AGL Energy took it to 100% ownership in April 2024.
- Corporate change: on 11 May 2026 OVO agreed the sale of this UK retail business to E.ON (and Home Services to Hometree), subject to regulatory approval. The Kaluza platform is explicitly excluded from the sale.

## Where GB energy data actually comes from

OVO publishes no open market data, which is structurally normal for a GB retailer. Open grid, market and system data in Britain comes from NESO, Elexon and the DNOs. A customer wanting their own smart-meter data programmatically can go around OVO entirely via a DCC "Other User" service such as n3rgy, Bright/Hildebrand or Loop.
